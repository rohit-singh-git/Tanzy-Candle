HOW WE STARTED

A kitchen counter, a saucepan, and too much time

Tanzy began the way most good things do — with too much time, a saucepan, and a stubborn belief that candles from a big-box shelf never smelled like anything at all. We started melting soy wax on our own stove, testing scent after scent until one finally felt like home.

Every candle we sell is still poured by hand, cooled slowly, and checked one by one before it leaves us. No shortcuts, no factories — just the same care as that very first batch, now shared a little wider.

HOW IT'S MADE

Four steps, no shortcuts

i.

Melt

Soy wax flakes are melted low and slow, never rushed, to keep the burn clean and even.

ii.

Scent

Skin-safe fragrance and essential oils are blended in by hand, batch-tested for balance.

iii.

Pour

Poured into vessels at the exact right temperature, then left to cure undisturbed.

iv.

Cure

Each candle rests up to two weeks so the scent settles fully before it reaches you.
